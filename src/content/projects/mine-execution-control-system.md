Client work for a mining contractor, delivered at Insig Technologies.

## Problem

Mine execution planners and control room operators need to plan mining activities and record their progress and outcomes.

## What I built

- REST APIs in C# and ASP.NET Core, on a microservice architecture, for the front end to consume and manipulate data.
- An API gateway to standardise and centralise the delivery of services through the microservices.
- The front end in Vue.js.
- Unit tests with xUnit for the APIs.
