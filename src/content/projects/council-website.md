Client work for a Perth local government, delivered at Diversus.

## What I built

Front-end and back-end development as one of the developers on the website: Next.js with Material UI on the front end, Contentful as the headless CMS for managing data, AWS Cognito for federated authentication and AWS Amplify for CI/CD deployment. I also worked on accessibility and on metadata for SEO.
