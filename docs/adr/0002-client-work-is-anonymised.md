# Client work is anonymised

Client work is published without naming the Client and without screenshots of the Client's systems; it is illustrated with architecture diagrams instead, because naming a Client or showing its systems needs permission from the employer and the Client. Each Client is referred to by a short neutral description instead (for example, "a WA fisheries industry body"). No Client's name may appear anywhere in this repo, including docs, tickets and commit messages. Project write-ups are drawn only from Kishor's résumé and his employer's staff profile, stated factually and not expanded beyond what those sources say.
